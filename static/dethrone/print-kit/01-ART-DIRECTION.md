# 01 · Art Direction — The Cursed Throne (V3b style bible)

Prime Fable 5 with this file **once at the start of the session**. Then prepend
the **STYLE BLOCK** (bottom of this file) to every image prompt so the whole
edition is visually consistent.

---

## 1. The one-line vision

> *"The referee's ledger by candlelight."* A game of quiet treason in a rotting
> medieval court, told through **aged parchment, warm ink, antique gold for
> royalty, and wax-seal red for corruption.**

The look is **hand-made and old**: illuminated-manuscript margins, ledger ruling,
wax seals, gilt edges, engraved/woodcut illustration — not glossy digital
fantasy, not cartoon, not photoreal.

---

## 2. Mood & genre

- **Setting:** a small medieval kingdom slowly being corrupted from within.
- **Tone:** tense, secretive, a little sinister; gallows humour in the flavour text.
- **Reference feel:** an illuminated manuscript crossed with an 18th-century
  ledger and a tarot deck; candlelit tavern; wax-sealed royal decrees.
- **Lighting:** low, warm candlelight from one side; deep but warm shadows; soft
  vignette darkening toward the corners.

---

---

## 4. Typography

- **Titles / headings:** a warm **old-style serif** — *Iowan Old Style, Palatino
  Linotype, Palatino, Book Antiqua, or Georgia*. High contrast, slightly
  condensed caps for card names, generous letter-spacing on small labels.
- **Body / rules text:** the same serif family, regular weight, ink `#2A2014`.
- **Flavour text:** italic serif, ink-soft `#5B4C38`.
- **No sans-serif, no modern/geometric fonts, no script fonts** on printed pieces.
- Numbers/values (duel value, cost) sit inside **gold or wax roundels/seals**.

---

## 5. Materials, texture & finish

- **Parchment grain** across every surface; slight age spots and edge darkening.
- **Hand-inked linework**: engraving / woodcut / pen-and-ink illustration style.
- **Gilt / gold-foil look** for royal frames, corner flourishes, and seals.
- **Wax seals** (red `#8C2F23`) used as timing/consequence stamps and signatures.
- **Ledger ruling** (faint horizontal lines) acceptable as a background texture.
- Subtle **drop shadow / emboss** on plaques and seals for depth. Keep it subtle.

---

## 6. Illustration style rules

- **Do:** engraved/woodcut illustration, illuminated-manuscript motifs, heraldry,
  tarot-like symbolic single subjects, candlelit interiors, aged maps.
- **Don't:** photorealism, 3D renders, anime/cartoon, neon, lens flares, modern
  clothing/objects, visible brush-stroke "digital painting" that fights the
  engraved look, or text baked into the illustration area (titles are set in the
  card frame, not painted into the scene).
- **Faces:** period-appropriate, painterly-but-restrained; expressive silhouettes
  read better than fine detail at card size.
- **Symbolism over gore:** corruption is shown with moss, cracks, rot, and
  cursed-maroon glow — not blood or graphic violence.

## 7. Consistency system (memorise this)

Two accent systems keep the whole set coherent:

**Role families → accent colour** (used on role card frames/footers):

| Family | Accent | Feel |
|--------|--------|------|
| Royal (King, Queen) | `#8C2F23` wax-red + heavy gold | Regal, gilded |
| Cursed (Cursed One) | `#6B2420` maroon | Dark, rotten, secret |
| Succession (heirs) | `#5A6E3A` moss | Ambitious, creeping |
| Knight | `#4A5568` slate | Cold steel, martial |
| Guard | `#5B4C38` ink-soft | Stolid, dutiful |
| Thief / Spy | `#3A3530` charcoal | Shadowy |
| Advisor | `#2A2014` ink | Scholarly, quiet power |

**Action decks → accent colour** (used on action card frames/footers):

| Deck | Accent | Theme |
|------|--------|-------|
| Market | `#5B4C38` | Trade, tools, movement |
| Tavern | `#5B4C38` | Rumours, gossip, shady deals |
| Knowledge | `#2A2014` | Investigation, records, votes |
| Barracks | `#4A5568` | Duels, force, protection |
| Graveyard | `#6B2420` | Dark power, corruption, desperate gold |
| Royal | `#8C2F23` | Crown authority, decrees |

**Timing seals** (small wax stamp on action/role cards):

| Timing | Seal letter | Seal colour |
|--------|:-----------:|-------------|
| On your turn | `T` | gold `#C79A3A` |
| Movement | `M` | moss `#5A6E3A` |
| Reaction | `R` | slate `#4A5568` |
| Duel | `D` | ink `#2A2014` |
| Vote | `V` | wax-red `#8C2F23` |
| Manual / table | `!` | ink `#5B4C38` |

## 8. THE STYLE BLOCK (copy-paste this before every prompt)

```
STYLE — The Cursed Throne (V3b):
Aged parchment / illuminated-manuscript board-game art, "referee's ledger by
candlelight." Warm cream paper (#F4ECD6) surface with fine grain and darkened,
lightly foxed edges; low warm candlelight and a soft corner vignette. Engraved /
woodcut / pen-and-ink illustration with hand-inked linework and antique
gold-foil accents (#A8842C, highlight #C79A3A). Corruption and dark magic shown
in wax-seal red (#8C2F23) and cursed maroon (#6B2420); graveyard/growth in moss
green (#5A6E3A). All type is a warm old-style serif (Iowan Old Style / Palatino /
Georgia); ink colour #2A2014. Values and stamps sit inside gold or red wax seals.
Symbolic, tarot-like single subjects; period medieval court. NO photorealism, NO
3D render, NO anime/cartoon, NO neon, NO modern objects, NO text painted inside
the illustration area. Colours warm and slightly desaturated, never glossy.
```

Then add the component template (from the phase file) and the specific subject.
