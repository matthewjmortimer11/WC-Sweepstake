# Cover Story beta playtest checklist

## Tables to run

- 4 players: one short round with Classic only, 5 minute timer.
- 6 players: Classic + Luxury + Chaos, 7 minute timer.
- 8 players: add Football or Weird, 7 minute timer.
- 12 players: Remote view, Classic + Chaos + Family, 10 minute timer.

## Observe

- Can a new host create and share a room in under 20 seconds?
- Does every player understand their private dossier within 5 seconds?
- Does the sealed dossier prevent accidental peeking at the table?
- Are the question prompts helpful without feeling bossy?
- Does the accusation phase make the final decision clearer?
- Do disconnected players and host transfer feel recoverable?

## Record

- Player count, selected packs, timer length, and whether the round completed.
- Confusing location names or prompts.
- Any mobile layout issue below 390px wide.
- Any moment where players needed rules explained twice.
- Whether the group voluntarily starts another round.
- Submit the notes through the in-app "Send playtest notes" form, or POST to `/coverstory/api/playtests`.

## Beta exit bar

- 95% of playtest rounds complete without state bugs.
- First room creation and join flow succeeds for every participant.
- At least 50 locations feel playable, with no repeated confusing reports.
- Table and Remote views are both readable on phone screens.
